# Onboarding Flow v2

## Steps

1. **Cadastro de Contas** — Add financial accounts
2. **Estratégia da Carteira** — Choose FREE_ALLOCATION or ASSET_CLASS_ALLOCATION (NEW)
3. **Projeto de Carteira** — Define asset targets (was step 2)
4. **Primeiro Aporte** — First contribution (was step 3)

## Step 2 UX

Two cards:
- **Livre (Recomendado)** — badge "Recomendado", flat allocation
- **Por Classe de Ativo** — hierarchical allocation (foundation only in Sprint 2.5)

Strategy is persisted to PortfolioTemplate.strategy.
